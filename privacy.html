PrismCap privacy

Effective: 14 October 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

PrismCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Profile, XP, preferences, favourites, and recent-play data in localStorage.
  • Optional haptics via the browser Vibration API (local only; never leaves the device).
  • Service worker cache of the app shell (same-origin static files).

What leaves your device

Nothing for gameplay, saves, or analytics. There is no Capricorn sync server and no third-party API calls from the app shell (connect-src 'self').

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
GitHub Pages (static host) Normal browser request metadata (IP, user agent) for HTML/JS/CSS/icons Serve the PWA First visit and when the service worker refreshes cached assets

After assets are cached, core play works fully offline. Clearing site data or removing the PWA deletes local saves.

Your controls

Export or reset progress from Settings where offered. Erase all data by clearing this site’s storage in the browser, or uninstalling the PWA.

Children

PrismCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. Supervise younger players for pass-and-play sessions.
